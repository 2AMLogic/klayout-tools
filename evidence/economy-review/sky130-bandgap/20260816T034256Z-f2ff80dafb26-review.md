## Judgment

The block's own `blocks/sky130-bandgap/NOTE.md` already records ground
truth independent of this review: **"area is over the ratified budget
pending DR-007 (relaxed from 0.05 to fit the drawn `MCC` cap)"** — i.e. the
spec's area budget line itself had to be *loosened* to accommodate this
layout, the strongest possible signal that this is the known-loose example.
This review's metrics corroborate that independently:

- **Left/right dead margins (~41 um each, symmetric)** — per the rubric's
  step 2 ("empty region at the outer margin, no adjacent guard ring/
  isolation structure"): inspecting the overview and the NW/SW/NE/SE
  quadrant renders, the left and right edges show routing/via layers
  (66/20, 67/20) thinning out well before the drawn bbox edge, with no
  guard-ring or deep-nwell isolation geometry occupying that band — this
  reads as **unused routing-channel margin left over from floorplanning**,
  not legitimate isolation spacing. Waste.
- **R1/R2 (left/right mid-height empty cells)** — same finding at finer
  grid resolution: both sit inside the dead-margin bands above, not
  adjacent to any matched-pair or guard-ring structure visible in the
  quadrant renders. Waste, same root cause as the margins.
- **Bottom margin (~37.6 um) vs. top margin (0 um)** — asymmetric, which
  the render confirms: the core's own I/O/bias routing concentrates toward
  the top edge (visible as denser 66/20 metal in the NW/NE quadrant
  renders), while the bottom edge is comparatively bare. This asymmetry
  itself is a floorplan-shape smell (rubric step 4) worth noting even
  though it isn't independently disqualifying here — the ratified-budget
  overage above is already the primary finding.
- Utilization (0.38) alone sits inside the isolation-heavy analog range and
  would not, by itself, have triggered a `revise` — this is exactly why the
  rubric requires checking margins/empty-regions and the spec's own budget
  line, not utilization in isolation.

## Verdict

**revise**

R1. Left margin (x=-10.0 to ~31.0 um, full height band, densest at
    y=140.3-177.9 um) is empty with no adjacent guard ring or matched
    device visible in the quadrant renders — pull the leftmost placed
    devices/routing inward by ~35-40 um, or place currently-unplaced
    circuitry (e.g. a bias-generation sub-block) into that band.
R2. Right margin (x=277.2 to 318.2 um, same y-band) — same finding,
    mirrored; same remedy.
R3. Re-run this review after a re-floorplan and confirm utilization moves
    toward the upper half of the 0.30-0.50 isolation-heavy range with the
    dead margins reduced, before treating DR-007's budget relaxation as
    the accepted final area rather than a temporary allowance.

Ratification of any area-budget change stays the operator's call; this
review is an opinion backed by independently-computed numbers.
