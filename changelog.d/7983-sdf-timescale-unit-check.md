- `klt functional-verification` with `options.sdf` now refuses, before the build, an SDF whose `TIMESCALE` differs from the time unit of a module that receives a delay, or whose unit cannot be established. Icarus applies each SDF delay in the annotated module's own unit and ignores the file's `TIMESCALE`, so a `1ps` file on `1ns` cell models annotated every delay 1000x too large while the report said `annotated: true`. A matching run records the SDF's `TIMESCALE` and the unit its delays were applied in as the additive `environment.sdf.timescale` and `environment.sdf.time_unit` on both the cocotb and Verilog-bench paths (#7983).
